Universal SDK (v2) · vanilla JavaScript

Each page is one scenario: one HTML file, one JS file, no framework. Managed = Yuno creates the payment. Manual = your server creates it from the one-time token.

  • Card form · managedSDK renders the card form and creates the payment.
  • Card form · manualSDK renders the form, your server creates the payment in createPayment.
  • Card form · your own pay buttonshowPayButton: false and transaction.submit().
  • Payment method list · managedStandalone list, select, then start().
  • Payment method list · manualSame list with createPayment.
  • Wallet buttonsApple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal buttons in your own layout.
  • HeadlessNo SDK UI: you pass the card data to start().
  • Secure fieldsPCI-isolated card fields inside your own form.
  • EnrollmentSave a card with a customer session instead of a checkout session.
  • Your own loadershowLoading replaces the SDK overlay.

Add ?country=BR&language=pt to any page to change the session country and the SDK language.
